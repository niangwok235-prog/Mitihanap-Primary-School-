ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Education Is The Foundation Of A Better Future.

Mitihanap Primary School is an educational institution committed to the academic, social and personal development of children.

We recognise that education is an important foundation for individual growth, community development and the future of Papua New Guinea.

Through effective teaching, student participation, discipline and cooperation with parents, we aim to create an environment where every learner can grow in knowledge, skills and positive values.

OUR EDUCATIONAL DIRECTION

Vision, Mission & Objectives

The principles that guide our responsibilities and educational goals.

◎
01 / OUR VISION

Our Vision

To develop educated, confident, disciplined and responsible young citizens who contribute positively to their families, communities and the future of Papua New Guinea.

✦
02 / OUR MISSION

Our Mission

To provide quality primary education through effective teaching, meaningful learning, good discipline, inclusive participation and cooperation between teachers, students, parents and the community.

⌁
03 / OUR OBJECTIVES

Our Objectives

To improve literacy, numeracy, creativity, critical thinking, personal development and positive behaviour while preparing students for further education.
